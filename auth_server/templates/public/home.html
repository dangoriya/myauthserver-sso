Identity & access control

One account. Every app on dilipdangoriya.com.np.

The IAM Auth Server is the central sign-in for the dilipdangoriya.com.np family of applications. Sign in once with your Google account or a local password, and jump between the calendar, live dashboards, portfolio tools, and every other internal app without re-entering your credentials.

What's inside the auth server

Everything a small set of internal apps needs to recognise the same person — without each app building its own login system.

🔐

Single sign-on (OIDC)

OpenID Connect at auth.dilipdangoriya.com.np. Every connected app redirects here to authenticate, then returns the user with a signed identity token.

🪪

Google social sign-in

Sign in with Google. We only request your name, email and profile picture — nothing more.

🔑

Local password & 2FA

Prefer not to use Google? Create a local account with email + password and optionally enable TOTP-based two-factor authentication.

🛡️

Centralised 2FA management

Forgotten your authenticator, lost your phone, or want to reset your 2FA secret? Reset it from the Profile page with an email-confirmed security code.

📨

Email-verified password reset

Forgot your password? Request a one-time reset link sent to your email and choose a new one in a couple of clicks.

🧩

OpenID for new apps

Add a new internal app by registering it as a client. It immediately gets sign-in, profile, and 2FA — no extra work for the app.

How the pieces fit together

The auth server sits in the middle. Users come from Google or a local password; apps trust the signed token issued by the auth server.
